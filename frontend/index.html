Transparent Weighted Scoring

Every option is scored using your chosen weights. Below is a realistic example of comparing career paths.

Illustrative Example Decision: Career Path Selection
Weights total: 100%

Criteria defined: Career Growth (40%), Personal Interest (30%), Ease of Learning (20%), Affordability (10%).

Alternative Career Growth (40%) Personal Interest (30%) Ease of Learning (20%) Affordability (10%) Final Score Status
Cloud Systems Engineer 8 / 10 9 / 10 7 / 10 8 / 10 8.10 Rank 1
Full-Stack Product Dev 9 / 10 8 / 10 6 / 10 7 / 10 7.90 Rank 2
Specialist Consultant 7 / 10 6 / 10 5 / 10 6 / 10 6.20 Rank 3
Transparent Formula: Score = Σ [(Weight × Rating) / 100]. For Cloud Systems Engineer:
(0.40 × 8) + (0.30 × 9) + (0.20 × 7) + (0.10 × 8) = 8.10. Numerical scores show how well options match your stated priorities—they do not replace human judgment.
